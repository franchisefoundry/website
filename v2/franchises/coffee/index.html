What we look at before recommending a coffee brand.

  1. 01
    Rent against trade
    Coffee margins live or die on rent. Ask for real P&Ls from sites like yours, not the best one.
  2. 02
    Weekday and weekend split
    Office-led sites go quiet on Saturdays. Make sure the model works on your footfall.
  3. 03
    Who supplies what
    Tied supply on beans and kit is normal. Know the prices before you sign.
